# Deprecated service models and replacement options

This guide compares replacement options for affected AI Quick Actions (AQUA) service models. Service-model deprecation concerns support in AQUA; it does not mean that the model publisher has discontinued the model.

Model deprecation, container retirement, and deletion are separate lifecycle events. Selecting or registering a replacement does not automatically migrate an existing Model Deployment or switch an application's endpoint. Any impact on existing deployments, including restart behavior, must be confirmed for the applicable service change.

## Replacement options

The table distinguishes options identified in the service catalog from additional models outside the catalog. A catalog listing alone does not establish that a replacement is available or validated for every shape, container, region, or realm. Confirm the exact model and deployment configuration in AQUA Model Explorer before migrating.

Outside-catalog options are candidates for separate compatibility validation, not approved AQUA service-model replacements. Benchmark results alone do not establish service support. If no suitable replacement is confirmed for your workload, contact support for an approved temporary or recovery path before changing the existing deployment.

## Before choosing a replacement

- **Availability and qualification:** Prefer a validated service-catalog replacement. Confirm its supported shape, container version, endpoints, and availability in your target region and realm. Consider an outside-catalog model only after confirming compatibility and a meaningful benefit for your workload.
- **Inference memory:** Memory figures above are approximate weights-only decimal GB. Total inference GPU memory is higher and depends on context length, image inputs, batch size, KV cache, adapters, and runtime overhead. For mixture-of-experts models, weight memory depends on total parameters, not only active parameters.
- **Benchmark comparison:** Scores are publisher-reported and may use different splits, prompts, or evaluation methods. Base and instruction-tuned results are not directly comparable. Family or BF16 scores may differ from the exact quantized checkpoint. The comparisons do not demonstrate equivalent accuracy or guaranteed performance gains.
- **Capabilities and request format:** Check image understanding, document and chart accuracy, languages, context length, tool calling, and reasoning output handling against your application. Base-to-instruct migrations change prompting and output behavior. Adapt chat templates, image preprocessing, and API request/response handling to the replacement.
- **Vision workloads:** Nemotron is a text-only reasoning option and cannot replace image inference. Granite is an option for document/chart understanding, not a demonstrated equivalent for multilingual chat or native tool calling. Phi's listed vision support is English, and its listed context limit is lower than Ministral's.
- **License terms:** Confirm the replacement model's license terms before use.

## Migration checklist

1. **Identify the existing deployment.** Record its exact model/checkpoint, container version, shape, region, endpoint contract, and inference settings. Confirm the applicable lifecycle notice and effective date.
2. **Choose a replacement.** Find the model in AQUA Model Explorer and confirm the supported model/container/shape combination for your region and realm. Review modality, base-versus-instruct behavior, language support, context limits, and resource requirements.
3. **Register and deploy.** Follow the [model registration guide](register-tips.md) and [model deployment guide](model-deployment-tips.md) to create and validate the replacement deployment.
4. **Test representative requests.** Use the [evaluation guide](evaluation-tips.md) where applicable and application-level tests. For text workloads, check chat formatting, response quality, tool calls, reasoning output, and required languages. For vision workloads, check image request encoding, document/chart questions, and image-dependent answer quality. Measure latency and memory under representative context lengths and concurrency.
5. **Switch after validation.** Update the application's endpoint and request/response handling only after the replacement meets your acceptance criteria. Registration and deployment alone do not move application traffic.
6. **Retain an approved recovery path.** Agree a supported fallback before switching, preserve the configuration needed to use it, and monitor the new deployment. Do not assume a deprecated model or retired container can be restarted or redeployed as a fallback.

For container-specific TGI migration instructions, see the [TGI deprecation guide](guide_to_upgrade_models_following_tgi_deprecation.md). Its restart-impact statement applies to that separate change and must not be generalized to the models in this guide.
